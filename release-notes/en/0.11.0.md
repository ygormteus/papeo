## New

- **Voice messages without delay:** in WhatsApp, recording starts the moment you tap the button. Before, on Mac, the first second of speech could be cut off.
- **Choose how to record:** in Settings → General → Voice messages, each option comes with a short explanation. **Instant** (default) records on tap with the microphone sound as it arrives; **Instant with Papeo cleanup** also removes background noise (fan, keyboard, street) and keeps your voice at a steady volume, on Mac and Windows; **macOS noise reduction** is the old way, with echo and noise handled by the system, but with the delay at the start.
- **Files tab:** links, attachments and downloads moved out of the System tab and got their own tab.

## Heads Up

- **On Mac, voice messages now record without echo cancellation by default.** If you record with sound playing through the speakers (no headphones), choose **Instant with Papeo cleanup** or **macOS noise reduction** in Settings → General → Voice messages. Voice and video calls don't change: they keep using the system's noise reduction.
