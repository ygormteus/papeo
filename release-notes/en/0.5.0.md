## New

- **Link preview:** in **Settings → Open links**, choose "In Papeo's preview" and links you click in chats open in a window over the app, without switching to your browser. The preview doesn't use a login or store anything; to continue, click **Open in browser**. It remembers the size you left it at.

## Improvements

- **Free up memory with confirmation:** the memory indicator at the top now asks before reloading accounts and shows which ones will be reloaded. You can check "Don't ask again" and turn it back on in Settings.

## Fixes

- **WhatsApp links:** clicking a link sent in a chat did nothing. Now it opens normally.
