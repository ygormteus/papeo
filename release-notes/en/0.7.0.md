## New

- **Saved replies:** add texts in **Settings → Saved replies** and, when replying from Home or the menu bar, type `/` to pick one.
- **Remind me:** in **Recent chats**, turn a message into a task linked to the chat, with the text already filled in.
- **Truly unread:** chats read in WhatsApp, in Papeo or on your phone leave the **Unread** list.

## Improvements

- **Link preview:** ⌘W (Ctrl+W on Windows) closes the preview even with the cursor on the page, there's a button to copy the link, and auto-lock doesn't count the time while you read the preview.
- **Update on Mac:** Papeo removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark on its own, so you no longer have to allow it in Privacy & Security after updating.

## Fixes

- **Quick reply from Home:** replying from recent chats no longer shows a permission error.
- **Self-hosted services on another port:** accounts with an address like `http://servidor:8080` now receive notifications.
