## New

- **Split view:** right-click an account in the sidebar and choose **Open side by side** to see two accounts at once: two WhatsApp accounts, two email accounts, or WhatsApp next to email. Drag the divider to resize each side; the top bar acts on the focused side (marked with a colored strip). **Close split view** is in the top bar.
- **Formatting in email:** the email reader's editor now has a formatting bar: text size, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, color, lists, quote, alignment and link (shortcuts ⌘B, ⌘I, ⌘U and ⌘K; Ctrl on Windows). Your signature shows up in the text and goes out with the logo.
- **Nextcloud Talk in Papeo:** Talk can open in Papeo's own reader, lighter than the Nextcloud page: conversations, messages, mentions, reactions, replies and attachments. Connect with **Connect to Talk** (approval opens in your browser, and Papeo never sees your password). Calls open in the browser.
- **Many emails at once:** in the email reader, select several messages with ⌘/Ctrl or Shift-click to archive, delete, move, mark as read or star them together. You can also drag messages onto a folder.
- **Email filters and shortcuts:** quick filters for unread, starred and with attachments; **Mark all as read**; **Undo** after archiving or moving; **Unsubscribe from this list** on newsletters; and keyboard shortcuts (`e` archive, `#` delete, `r` reply, `a` reply all, `f` forward, `s` star, `u` unread, `/` search).

## Improvements

- **Email opens faster:** the email list is kept on this computer and shows up right away; scroll to the end and older messages keep coming, without pages. Large emails open without downloading the attachments, which only come when you open or save one.
- **Email notification opens the message:** clicking a new-email notification opens that message directly.
- **Images from senders you trust:** **Always show from** a sender remembers your choice and shows their images next time.
- **Zoom in the reader:** the top bar zoom (and ⌘/Ctrl +, − and 0) now also works in the email and Talk readers.
- **Unread counts in the collapsed sidebar:** with the sidebar collapsed, each account's unread count shows on its icon, and the number pulses when a new message or email arrives.
- **Signature logo in the editor:** while writing an email, the signature with the logo shows as a preview, the way it will go out.
