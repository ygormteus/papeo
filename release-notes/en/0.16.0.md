## New

- **Nextcloud Talk:** in **Add service**, choose **Nextcloud Talk** and enter your company's Nextcloud address (the one you open in the browser). Papeo opens straight into your conversations, with the unread counter, notifications, calls and a signed-out warning.
- **Signature with a logo:** the email reader's signature now takes an image (your company logo), bold, italic, colors, sizes and links. Paste the signature you already use in Gmail or Outlook or build it in the editor; the email goes out formatted and the logo travels inside it.
- **Email attachments open on screen:** in the email reader, click an attachment's name to see it right away, without downloading. On the Mac, PDFs, images, Word, Excel and video open in a Papeo window; on Windows, PDFs, images, audio and video. In the window, **Save**, **Open in app** or **Show in folder**; when you close it, nothing is left in Downloads. The icon next to the attachment still saves it directly, and the space bar opens the message's first attachment.
- **Download all attachments:** with more than one attachment, **Download all** saves them at once into a folder named after the subject, in Downloads.
- **Suggested addresses:** when writing an email, To, Cc and Bcc suggest people you've written to or received email from. Use the arrow keys and Enter or Tab to complete. The addresses stay on this computer only.

## Improvements

- **Gmail and Outlook through the site or the reader:** when adding an email account, choose under **Open as** whether it opens through the site (with your usual sign-in) or through Papeo's email reader (IMAP). Before, Papeo went straight to IMAP.
- **Full recipients:** in an open email, click the sender or a recipient to see the address, **Write to** them or **Copy address**. With many recipients, the first three show up followed by **and N more**.
- **Folder names in your language:** Drafts, Sent, Trash, Spam, Archive, All mail and Starred show up in Papeo's language, even when the server sends them in another one.
- **Signature within reach:** set up your signature with the **Signature** button in the email reader or in **Settings → General → Email → Signatures**, which lists every account with email over IMAP.
