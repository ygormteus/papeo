## New

- **Mini window** in the menu bar icon (Mac) or system tray (Windows): see your latest chats and reply without opening Papeo.
- **Reply right from the notification** for WhatsApp and Telegram.
- **Discord** as its own service, with notifications and a mention counter.
- **Contact search** (`⌘ K` on Mac, `Ctrl K` on Windows): type a name and Papeo opens the chat.
- **Icon menu** with recent messages, your accounts, Do Not Disturb and lock.
- **What's New**: after every update, Papeo shows you this list.

## Improvements

- Clicking a notification opens exactly that chat.
- Papeo now checks for updates every 2 hours (it used to be once a day).
- Clearer wording for the option to switch to the notification's account when you come back.
