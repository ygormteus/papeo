## New

- **Papeo in English:** the whole app (screens, tray, Mac menu, notifications and error messages) now speaks English too. Choose in **Settings → General → Language**: System, Português or English. The change applies right away. If you already used Papeo, it stays in Portuguese until you change this option.
- **Slack and Microsoft Teams:** both appear in **Add service**, with their own icon and color. For Teams, choose a personal or a work or school account. Papeo tells you when the account signs out and allows the microphone and camera for calls.
- **Undo send:** in the email reader, an email waits a few seconds before going out, with an **Undo** button. Set the wait (off, 5, 10 or 20 seconds) in **Settings → General → Undo send**. If the app closes during the wait, the email isn't sent and goes back to the editor.
- **Email signature:** each email account can have a signature, added automatically to new messages, replies and forwards. Write it in the account's email settings, under **Signature**.
- **Drafts on the server:** the email editor saves the draft to the account's Drafts folder whenever you pause and when you close it. Drafts started on your phone or in webmail open in Papeo so you can finish them, and **Discard** deletes the draft.
- **Quiet summary:** when Do Not Disturb or quiet hours end, Papeo shows how many messages arrived in each account and in which chats, in a notification and a card on Home. Turn it off in **Settings → Notifications → Summary when silence ends**.
- **What's new in English:** this screen also appears in the chosen language.

## Heads Up

- **After adding a Slack or Teams account, don't go back to an earlier version of Papeo.** The earlier version can't read the account list and opens with only the default accounts. When you update again, recover the list from the `services.json.corrompido` file in the settings folder.
