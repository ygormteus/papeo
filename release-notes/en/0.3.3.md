## New

- **Zoom at a glance:** with an account open, the top bar shows the **−** and **+** buttons and the current size. Click the percentage to return to actual size. Shortcuts still work (⌘/Ctrl + and ⌘/Ctrl −), and zoom is saved for each account.

## Fixes

- **Back from sleep:** after your computer was asleep, WhatsApp could get stuck on "Connecting to WhatsApp… Trying again" until you quit and reopened Papeo. Now, when it wakes, Papeo reloads your open accounts on its own a few seconds after the network returns.
