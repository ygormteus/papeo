## New

- **Settings in tabs:** General, Notifications, Privacy, System, Data and About, with the title and tabs always in view.
- **Search in settings:** in ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows), type something like "dark", "password" or "tray" and Papeo opens the right tab, shows the option and puts focus on it.
- **Version history:** What's New now also shows earlier versions, with a list alongside to browse.
- **Services in their own panel:** the services table (memory, notifications and hibernation) moved out of Home and opens when you click **N services** at the top.
- **Tasks on wide screens:** list on the left and, on the right, a summary by group, accounts with open tasks (click to filter) and notes.

## Improvements

- New, cleaner look in **Add service**, **What's New** and **Services**, and a smooth animation when windows open (turned off with "reduce motion").
- The top bar no longer breaks in narrow windows; the frozen-account alert highlights the reload button.
- With the sidebar collapsed, "not responding" shows in red and the account name shows its status on hover.

## Fixes

- Home showed "idle for null min" for accounts not yet opened.
- Screen readers said "1 overdue" and "1 unread" in the plural.
