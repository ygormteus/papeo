## New

- **Email as a service:** add **Gmail** or your **webmail** (Roundcube and others, by address) like any other service. The counter shows unread messages in the inbox, the open subject appears in recent chats and, for a task linked to an email, Papeo finds and opens the message.
- **Preview attachments before downloading:** in **Settings → Attachments**, choose **Preview** to open PDFs, spreadsheets, documents and images in their own window before saving. From there you can save to Downloads or open in the default app (Excel, Word, PDF reader) to edit; closing without saving deletes the temporary file. On Windows, PDFs and images appear in the window; Excel and Word open in their apps.
