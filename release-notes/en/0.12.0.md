## New

- **Lock with a Papeo PIN:** on computers without Windows Hello (or without Touch ID/a system password), you can lock Papeo with a PIN or password just for it, in Settings → Privacy. On Windows, a shortcut takes you to the sign-in options to create a Windows PIN, which already enables Windows Hello even without a camera or fingerprint reader. After a few wrong attempts, Papeo waits a bit before accepting another try. If you forget the PIN, you can unlock by signing out of all accounts (tasks on this computer are erased and sync is turned off).

## Improvements

- **Clearer sidebar:** the account status (disconnected, not responding, sleeping) appears under the name, with a dot on the icon, and unread counters are more subtle. In short windows, the list scrolls instead of squeezing the items.
- **Customize account:** live preview of how the account will look, easier-to-pick colors and emojis, and the Save button always in view.
