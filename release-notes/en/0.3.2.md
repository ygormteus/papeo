## Improvements

- **Opens faster:** the first account in the sidebar loads right away and the others follow, one at a time, without competing with the app launch.
- **Lighter when you return:** after your computer has been idle for 20 minutes, Papeo reloads accounts that have built up memory, so everything is light when you come back. Notifications keep working.
- Automatic reload never happens during calls, while the microphone, camera or screen sharing is in use, or while audio or video is playing.
