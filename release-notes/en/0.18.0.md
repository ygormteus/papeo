## New

- **Account in a separate window:** right-click an account and choose **Open in separate window** (or use the button in the top bar) to move the account into its own window, which you can put on another monitor. WhatsApp and other sites move over without reloading; the email and Talk readers work too. Close the window, or choose **Back to the main window**, to bring the account back.
- **Easier split view:** drag an account from the sidebar onto the left or right side of the screen to open both side by side. You can also use the **Split screen** button in the top bar, or **Split the screen with…** in the open account's menu.

## Fixes

- **Email in the reader no longer shows as hibernating:** on Home and in the sidebar, an email account in Papeo's reader shows its unread count and connection state instead of "Hibernating: open to refresh".
