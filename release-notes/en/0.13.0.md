## New

- **Papeo email reader:** email accounts open in a reader of their own, much lighter than the website. You can read, search, reply, forward, write with attachments, archive, mark as spam, delete and star, in all folders. The website only loads when you click "Open site" (and unloads when you go back to the reader). Remote images stay blocked until you ask, so you aren't tracked.
- **Outlook with Microsoft sign-in:** Outlook accounts (personal and Microsoft 365) sign in with Microsoft in your browser; your password never passes through Papeo.
- **Gmail and other providers over IMAP:** Gmail (with an app password), Hostinger, Locaweb, KingHost, UOL, BOL, Terra, Yahoo, iCloud or any IMAP server. When you add "Other email", just the address and provider are needed; the password comes in the next step.
- **Email alerts without the site open:** sender, subject and unread counter come straight from the server; clicking the alert opens the email in the reader.
- **Outlook as a service:** new account type, personal or work/school.

## Fixes

- **WhatsApp stuck loading:** after hours with the computer asleep, WhatsApp could stay on the loading screen. Now the account is reopened from scratch when you return (and on reload).
- **Protected configuration:** an unreadable accounts file is no longer overwritten; it's kept alongside.
- Several fixes for memory, sync, backup, tasks and window focus.
