## Fixes

- **Update on Mac:** if your Mac opens Papeo from a temporary copy (this happens when the app is still marked as downloaded from the internet), the update failed with "Read-only file system". Now Papeo warns you and shows what to do: quit the app and run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Papeo.app` in Terminal. If it's open straight from the installer, it asks you to drag it to the Applications folder.
