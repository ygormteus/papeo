## New

- **Work and personal:** mark each account as **Work** or **Personal** (in **Customize**) and choose in **Settings → Work and personal** which ones show: all, work only, personal only or **by work hours** (days and times). Outside the mode, accounts disappear from the sidebar, don't notify and don't count toward unread; you can hibernate those accounts too. Switch in the sidebar, in the menu bar/tray or with ⌘⇧M (Ctrl+Shift+M on Windows).
- **Reply later:** in recent chats (Home and menu bar), choose when you want to be reminded to reply — in 1 h, today at 6 PM, tomorrow, Monday or at the start of work hours. Notifications also gained a **Remind in 1 h** button.
- **Wrong-account alert:** the open account gets a frame in its color, its name and context flash at the top when you switch accounts, and quick reply shows which account the message will be sent from. The frame can be turned off.

## Improvements

- **Lighter:** data is saved without freezing the interface, and there's less repeated background work.

## Fixes

- The open chat no longer jumps to the bottom on every update, and "X min ago" updates on its own again.
- Your selection in the saved replies list no longer jumps back to the top.
- With the sidebar collapsed, an account's context menu is no longer hidden behind the site.
