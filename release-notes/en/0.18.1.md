## Fixes

- **Dragging to split the screen works on Mac:** when you drag an account from the sidebar over an open site (like WhatsApp), the side where it will open now lights up, and dropping it puts both accounts side by side. Before, the screen went back to a single account. The account's name follows the cursor while dragging, and **Esc** cancels.
- **Email editor:** the "⌘ Enter sends" hint is no longer cut off when the panel is narrow.
