## New

- **Unread only:** in **Recent chats** on Home, choose **Unread** to see only chats with new messages. A chat counts as read when you open it in Papeo or reply from here. Your choice is saved.

## Improvements

- **Cleaner Home:** the services list starts collapsed, showing only a summary (accounts, memory and unread). Click **Services** to expand it.

## Fixes

- **WhatsApp links:** clicking a link sent in a chat now really opens, in your browser or in Papeo's preview, and only once.
- **Update on Mac:** after updating, your Mac could open Papeo from a temporary copy again, and the next update failed with "Read-only file system". Papeo now avoids this on its own.
