## New

- **Email attachments open on screen:** in the email reader, click an attachment's name to see it right away, without downloading. On the Mac, PDFs, images, Word, Excel and video open in a Papeo window; on Windows, PDFs, images, audio and video. In the window, **Save**, **Open in app** or **Show in folder**; when you close it, nothing is left in Downloads. The icon next to the attachment still saves it directly, and the space bar opens the message's first attachment.

## Improvements

- **Signature within reach:** set up your signature with the **Signature** button in the email reader or in **Settings → General → Email → Signatures**, which lists every account with email over IMAP.
