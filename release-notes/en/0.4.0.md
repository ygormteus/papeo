## New

- **Tasks and reminders linked to chats:** while chatting with someone, click the notepad in the top bar (or ⌘/Ctrl ⇧ N) and create a task right there. At the set time you get a reminder, and one click opens that chat in the right account. You can also create standalone tasks, with no chat.
- **Notes per contact:** a notepad next to each chat to keep preferences, agreements and customer history. It saves automatically.
- **Tasks page and "For today":** overdue, today and upcoming, with a filter by account; Home shows what's due today and the menu bar mini window shows your next reminders.
- **Dates in text:** end a task with a date in Portuguese, like "retornar orçamento amanhã 15h", "pagar boleto dia 5" or "ligar em 30 min", and Papeo understands when to remind you.
- **Repeating tasks:** every day, weekdays, every week or every month.
- **Zoom at a glance:** − and + buttons in the top bar to make chats larger or smaller.

## Improvements

- In a reminder, **Snooze 1 h** and **Complete** work right from the notification.
- Search (⌘/Ctrl K) also finds tasks and notes.
- Backup now includes tasks and notes.

## Fixes

- **Back from sleep:** after your computer was asleep, WhatsApp could get stuck on "Connecting… Trying again". Now, when it wakes, Papeo reloads your open accounts on its own.
